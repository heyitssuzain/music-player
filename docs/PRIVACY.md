# AURA Android — Privacy Policy & Permissions Notice

**Zero Telemetry. Zero Tracker SDKs. Zero Data Harvesting.**

1. **Local Storage First:** All music metadata, playlists, history, and preferences are stored exclusively on your device inside a local SQLite (Room) database.
2. **Audio Recordings:** Voice commands are processed in real-time through on-device speech recognition. Audio waveforms are never saved, uploaded, or transmitted to any remote servers.
3. **No Account Required:** AURA operates 100% anonymously without requiring an account, email, or login credentials.
4. **Permissions:**
   - `RECORD_AUDIO`: Strictly used during push-to-talk voice commands.
   - `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`: Strictly used to scan local audio files in your library.
   - `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` & `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`: Strictly used to display active media playback notifications with lock-screen controls.
